About

Reliable. Respectful.
Professional.

Ikraam Sadek is a Junior Lecturer at Belgium Campus ITversity, with a teaching career that began in 2023 following the completion of his studies and an AIT internship. Driven by a natural aptitude for transferring knowledge, he found his calling watching students grasp concepts better than he initially did himself.

His classroom is a space of engagement, discussion, and humor — a deliberate approach to keeping students present and thinking. He firmly believes that thorough comprehension, not just surface exposure, is the true measure of learning.

⚡ Professional Superpower

Helpfulness without limits — willing to assist any student, at any time, the moment a knowledge gap appears. It's not a duty; it's a reflex.
